# Moments I Love — Annotated Reference Log

A growing log of **specific moments in tracks** that move me. Not "this is a good track" — *"bar X to Y, here's what makes it work, here's what I want to learn from it."*

When stuck, this is the wellspring. Better than browsing Splice randomly.

## Seed entries (from the user's DJ set — bar marks approximate)

### Ezequiel Arias — Prisma (Extended Mix)
- **Moment:** ~3:00 — the way the pluck delays start folding into the pad as the breakdown approaches
- **What makes it work:** the delays decrease in feedback while the pad swells — creates the impression that the pluck is *dissolving into* the pad
- **Technique guess:** automated delay feedback (descending from 50% to 0% over 16 bars) + sidechained pad volume rising in mirror
- **My version:** try this on K-004 (pluck) → K-008 (pad) at the same transition point in a new track

### Rauschhaus — Nightshift
- **Moment:** ~1:30 — the bass dropout where only kick + percussion remain for 8 bars before bass returns
- **What makes it work:** absence creates ANTICIPATION. The percussion shines without the bass eating headroom.
- **Technique:** mute the bass track for exactly 8 bars at a transition. Add a build-up element (the breath, per [[SIGNATURE-MOVE]]) in the last bar
- **My version:** every track should have one moment of bass dropout

### Guy J — Silver Lake
- **Moment:** ~5:00 final breakdown — the pad chord change against the held sub
- **What makes it work:** harmonic tension. The sub stays on the tonic while the pad moves through a IV → bVI sequence. Creates emotional weight.
- **Technique:** sub on root (e.g. A1) sustained whole-bar notes, pads play Dm → F over the held A
- **My version:** try a held sub + moving pad chord progression on a melodic techno track

### DAVI — Self CNTRL
- **Moment:** the percussion polyrhythm intro — congas + shaker at offset
- **What makes it work:** the 3-against-4 polyrhythm between congas (triplet) and shakers (16ths) creates the hypnotic pull
- **Technique:** intentional polyrhythmic offset, NOT same-grid percussion
- **My version:** lift this in afro-tech or tribal-techno experiments

### Matan Caspi — Wind Pipe
- **Moment:** the lead synth's slow filter sweep over 16 bars — the build-into-drop arc
- **What makes it work:** patience. Modern producers rush builds in 4-8 bars. Matan takes 16.
- **Technique:** automate filter cutoff over 16 bars (not 4), with the rate increasing slightly toward the end
- **My version:** never automate a build in less than 8 bars. Try 16 in every track.

### Above & Beyond — Sun In Your Eyes
- **Moment:** ~3:30 vocal entry over the rebuild
- **What makes it work:** the vocal is HIGH in the mix and slightly forward — it's the emotional payoff after a long instrumental build
- **Technique:** vocal at -3 dB during entry (loud!), let it pull back to -8 dB once the listener has registered it
- **My version:** if a track has a vocal, the first entry should be PROMINENT, then settle

## How to use this file

When starting a new track and stuck:
1. Scroll this list
2. Find a moment that triggers something
3. Identify the technique
4. Try a variation of it in your track
5. Cross-reference [[ARTIST-PROFILE]] cross-genre calendar to pick which lane

When you discover a new moment you love:
1. Add an entry below following the format
2. Don't worry about perfect technique-guessing — the *act of articulating what you love* is most of the value
3. Try to spread entries across genres so you have inspiration sources for off-lane experiments

## Entry template

```markdown
### [Artist] — [Track]
- **Moment:** [timestamp or bar range] — [one-sentence description]
- **What makes it work:** [the emotional/structural reason — not the technical one yet]
- **Technique guess:** [your best attempt at how it was made — even if wrong]
- **My version:** [how you could use this in your own work]
```

## Adding entries — discipline

- Target: **1 new entry per week**
- Source: anything — your own catalog, new releases you discover, old favorites, even non-electronic music
- The bar isn't "huge moment" — it's "I love this specific thing"
- Even an entry per month builds up to 12-50 entries a year — a real wellspring
