# Melodic House

## Genre Overview

Melodic house emerged in the mid-2010s as a polished, emotionally-rich variant of deep and progressive house, characterized by lush harmonic content, memorable arpeggiated synth hooks, and a focus on euphoric build-and-release dynamics. Distinct from "melodic techno" (which leans darker and 124-126 BPM) and "progressive house" (which is longer-form and more hypnotic), melodic house sits in the brighter, more accessible zone — closer to mainstream consumption while retaining underground credibility. The genre's spiritual home is the Anjunadeep label (founded 2010 by Above & Beyond), with labels like All Day I Dream, Last Night On Earth, and Bedrock contributing to the sound. Lane 8, Yotto, Tinlicker, Ben Böhmer, and Nora En Pure are emblematic. The sound spans warm sunset-vibes (Nora En Pure) through cinematic atmospheric (Ben Böhmer) to club-driving (Yotto).

## Characteristics

- **Instrumentation**: Lush analog-style pads, arpeggiated synth sequences (often plucks), warm sub bass, piano stabs, acoustic textures (guitar, strings — sometimes live, often sampled), four-on-the-floor kick (less driving than techno), shaker/conga percussion for warmth, soft vocal hooks.
- **Vocals**: When present, ethereal and emotional. Female vocalists dominate (Adriana Proenza, Run Rivers, Andrea Caccese collabs). Often vocoder-treated. Sometimes male falsetto. Lyrics tend toward universal themes — love, longing, journey, transcendence.
- **Production**: Bright, polished mixes. Wide stereo image. Heavy sidechain ducking for breathing room. Long reverb tails and dotted delays create depth. Sub bass is clean and tonal. Mid-range emphasis on the arp/pluck and pad sweet spot.
- **Energy/Mood**: Warm, emotive, euphoric. Sunset-to-night-time energy. Less "peak-time" than progressive house, more "journey." Listener-friendly without being commercial.
- **Structure**: 5-8 minute tracks. Intro of 32-64 bars with pad + bass + light percussion. Drop returns to the arp + kick. Breakdown around 3-4 min strips to pad + vocal hook. Final build adds full percussion + lead.
- **Tempo**: **120-124 BPM** typical, occasionally up to 126. Steady four-on-the-floor.

## Production DNA — translate to decisions

- Tempo: **122 BPM** is the standard. 120 for chillier, 124 for clubbier
- Key: Major keys feature more here than in progressive house. C major, D major, A minor, G minor all common
- Kick: Soft-but-present. Not as dry as techno. Round bottom-end
- Bass: Tonal sub with occasional melodic movement. Sidechained
- Pluck/Arp: This is the genre's signature. Wavetable or analog synth, plucky envelope, heavy delay (1/4 dotted) + plate reverb. Browse user's library: **Datacode FOCUS** for prog-leaning, **Production Music Live Production Pack Vol.2** for melodic techno crossover
- Pads: Lush, layered (2-3 pad sources stacked). Slow filter movement. Long attack
- Vocal: Ethereal female hook 4-8 bars long, looped throughout
- Master: -10 to -8 LUFS for club, -12 to -14 for streaming
- Genre adjacent: This is the closest commercial cousin to user's set DNA. When user wants "more accessible" or "with a vocal hook", lean here from progressive-house
