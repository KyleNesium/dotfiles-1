# Melodic Techno

## Genre Overview

Melodic techno crystallized in the early-to-mid 2010s as Italian duo Tale Of Us (Carmine Conte and Matteo Milleri) and German producer Stephan Bodzin pushed techno toward more emotional, cinematic territory. The Afterlife label (founded by Tale Of Us in 2016) became the genre's primary institution, defining its aesthetic: dark but emotionally rich, modal harmonies, soaring lead synth lines, driving four-on-the-floor pulse, cinematic builds. The genre sits at the intersection where progressive house's melodic patience meets techno's rhythmic intensity — typically 122-126 BPM (slightly above prog), darker than melodic house, more emotional than driving techno. Modern flag-bearers include Mind Against, Mathame, Innellea, ARTBAT, Adriatique, Massano, Anyma (Matteo Milleri's solo project), CamelPhat, and Stephan Bodzin himself. The Afterlife events at Hï Ibiza, Burning Man, and arena tours have made melodic techno the dominant "premium electronic" sound of the late 2010s and 2020s.

## Characteristics

- **Instrumentation**: Driving four-on-the-floor kick (tighter than progressive, looser than peak techno). Layered arp + lead synths (Spire, Diva, Pigments, Phase Plant are the genre's signature tools). Sub bass that moves between tonal and percussive. Atmospheric pads with movement. Modular-sounding plucks. Vocal samples — often pitched, processed beyond recognition, used as texture.
- **Vocals**: Sparse and treated. When present, female ethereal vocals through heavy reverb and pitch-shifting. Rarely full lyrics — more often emotional fragments. Anyma's recent work pushes vocal use further (AI-generated voices).
- **Production**: Dark, cinematic, dense. Heavy sidechain pumping creates rhythmic pulse. Reverb everywhere — long plates and halls on pads/leads. Layered arps create the signature "movement." Mid-scoop EQ. Sub is clean and tonal.
- **Energy/Mood**: Emotional darkness — melancholy with build-up euphoria. "Cathartic" is the right word. Cinematic and grand without being cheesy. Designed for 1am-3am peak-time emotional moments at festival or club.
- **Structure**: 6-8 minute tracks. Intros build over 32-64 bars. Massive breakdowns at 3-4 min that strip away kick, leave lead synth and pad. Drops are melodic revelations.
- **Tempo**: **122-126 BPM**. 124 is the sweet spot. Driving four-on-the-floor.

## Production DNA — translate to decisions

- Tempo: **124 BPM**
- Key: Minor keys dominate (A minor, F minor, C minor, D minor). Sometimes modal (Phrygian, Dorian). Modal scales give the signature darker emotional feel
- Chord progressions: i - VI - III - VII (Am-F-C-G), i - iv - VI - V (Am-Dm-F-E), or modal vamps that don't fully resolve
- Kick: Tight, dry, punchy — closer to peak techno than prog. Layered with a sub
- Sub bass: Tonal, clean, sidechained
- Arp/Lead synth: THE genre's signature. Spire, Diva, Pigments. Bright but with rolloff. Heavy delay (1/4 dot) + plate reverb. Browse user's library: **Datacode FOCUS Diva**, **Melodic Techno Loops Vol 1-3**, **Production Music Live Production Pack Vol.2** are gold for this
- Pads: Dark, evolving. Layered with slow filter sweeps
- Vocal: Pitched fragments or single phrase loops. Use **DABRO Tekhaus** or process **W.A. Ethnic** vocals heavily
- FX: Reverse impacts, white noise risers, modular-sounding sweeps
- Master: -8 to -7 LUFS for club, -9 to -10 for DJ tools, -12 for streaming
