# Progressive Psy-Trance

## Genre Overview

Progressive psy-trance (also "prog psy" or "progressive psychedelic trance") is a subgenre of psy-trance that emerged in the early-to-mid 2000s as a slower, more groove-oriented variant of full-on psy-trance. Where full-on psy operates at 145-150 BPM with relentless build-and-drop intensity, progressive psy slows to **134-138 BPM** and emphasizes hypnotic, rolling basslines, deeper grooves, and longer atmospheric builds. The genre's spiritual home is the Israeli scene (Astrix's "Trance Mission" label, Iboga Records), the German psy scene (Iboga, Spin Twist), and Goa's enduring scene. Modern leaders include Astrix, Vini Vici (in their prog moods), Atmos, Ace Ventura, Liquid Soul, Yotopia, Ranji, Symbolic, Audiomatic, and Captain Hook. The genre sits between full-on psy's peak-time intensity and tech-trance's harder edge, with a hypnotic, journey-oriented quality.

## Characteristics

- **Instrumentation**: Driving four-on-the-floor kick (much faster than house — almost relentless), rolling 16th-note bassline (the defining feature — also known as "rolling bass"), acid lines (303-style), psychedelic FX (granular sweeps, white noise builds, vocal samples processed beyond recognition), atmospheric pads, occasional ethnic instrument samples.
- **Bass**: The 16th-note rolling bassline is THE signature. Driving, hypnotic, locked to the kick. Sidechain pumping creates the genre's pulse.
- **Vocals**: Almost never full songs. Vocal samples are spoken-word fragments (often from films, lectures, esoteric speakers), psychedelic chants, or pitched/granular textures. Used as atmospheric layers, not melodic content.
- **Production**: Loud, dense, designed for outdoor festival sound systems. Heavy sidechain. Bright, hi-fi mixes — emphasis on clarity at high volume. Reverb is used sparingly compared to other electronic genres — clarity > depth.
- **Energy/Mood**: Hypnotic, building, psychedelic. Designed for the second wave of energy at outdoor festivals (after the warmup, before peak full-on). Sunset-to-midnight territory at outdoor events. Communal, transcendent.
- **Structure**: 7-9 minute long-form tracks. Intros build over 64+ bars. Breakdowns strip to atmospheric layers + bass + kick. Builds typically include filter sweeps + arp intensification + impact FX. Drops are bassline-and-kick events.
- **Tempo**: **134-138 BPM**. 138 is the festival sweet spot. Faster than tech house (124), slower than full-on psy (145+).

## Production DNA — translate to decisions

- Tempo: **138 BPM** standard. 136 for chiller, 140 for harder
- Key: Minor keys dominate. F minor, A minor, C minor common. Modal also works (Phrygian = darker)
- Kick: Tight, dry, present. Lower fundamental than tech house. Layered with sub for weight
- Bass: THE genre defining element. 16th-note rolling pattern on the root, locked to kick. Use Serum 2 or DUNE 3 for the typical "saw + filter" sound. Heavy sidechain (kick triggers a sharp ducking envelope)
- Acid/lead: Optional 303-style acid line for tension. Use Live's Operator or Serum 2 with a square wave + filter envelope
- Pads: Dark, atmospheric, evolving. Less melodic than melodic techno
- FX: Risers, white noise builds, granular textures, vocal samples processed through grain delays

- Master: -7 to -6 LUFS — psy is loud. Festival systems can take it
