# Sample, Loop & Preset Library Index

**Root:** `D:\Presets, Samples and Loops` (mount: `/mnt/d/Presets, Samples and Loops`)

**Scale:** 70+ commercial packs · 60,138 WAV files · 1,324 MIDI files · 641 Serum presets · 334 Diva/u-he presets · 273 Kontakt (.nki) instruments.

The library skews **hard toward Melodic Techno, Progressive House, Deep House, Tech House** — perfectly matched to the Sudbeat / Asymmetric / Lost & Found set DNA. Plus huge bundles for Trance/Psy and broad Techno, and ethnic/world layers for organic textures.

> ⚠️ **Diva is installed but NOT licensed.** The 334 Diva presets and the Diva-based Melodic Techno packs are **locked** until Diva is purchased ($179). Until then, substitute with Live 12's stock synths: **Drift** (closest to Diva for analog bass/lead), **Analog** (poly pads), **Wavetable** (modulating textures), **Meld** (dual-osc). The Diva presets become unlocked the moment a Diva license is added — until then don't suggest them.

# Creative usage playbook

## For the **prog/melodic-techno template** we're building
1. **Bass — Rolling** → Datacode FOCUS Diva ("bass" presets) OR Melodic Techno Loops Vol.1 Diva bank → load into the Diva track, pick a 16th-note arp patch.
2. **Lead/Pluck** → Evolution Of Sound Deceiver Vol.6 Diva ("pluck" or "lead") → that's 185 presets to A/B from.
3. **Pads** → PML Production Pack Vol.2 Diva pad presets → slowest attack patch, send to ValhallaVintageVerb long plate.
4. **Loops as inspiration, not glue** — drop a True Samples or Ancore loop into a Session slot to feel the groove, then write your own MIDI on top.
5. **Vocal hooks** → DABRO Tekhaus Vocals for tech-leaning, SHARP Tech House Creatures for stabs, W.A. Ethnic EDM Vocals for the "Onire" colour.

## Cross-genre combos (the interesting stuff)
- **Indian sitar (Soltan / Organic Loops / Prime Loops Sarasvati) over a Diva prog bassline** → instant Anjunadeep "Sun In Your Eyes" energy.
- **Keepforest Watchkeeper risers/impacts** into Production Music Live builds → cinematic prog transitions, sounds like a label release.
- **Black Octopus Pure Analog Sweeps IV** as the build-up layer — automation-friendly white-noise risers, drop into the NOISE/RISER track in the template.
- **Akai 80s Nostalgia + Diva pad** → Prime Loops Synthwave territory but with prog tempo (122 BPM).
- **Audioreakt Minimal Techno `.als` template** — open as reference for how a minimal Ableton session is wired, steal routing ideas.

## Reading filename hints
Most loop packs encode BPM in filenames (e.g. `Loop_122_Am_BassRoll.wav`). Search by:
- `*122*` for our prog tempo
- `*120*`, `*124*` for tempo-stretchable neighbours
- `*Am*`, `*Cmin*` etc. for key
- Folder names like `Drums/`, `One Shots/`, `MIDI/`, `Diva Presets/` are reliable
