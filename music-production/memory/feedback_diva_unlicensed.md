When working in Ableton, do **not** load u-he Diva or suggest Diva-based presets — the user has Diva installed but unlicensed. Trying to use it will result in demo-mode noise/silence and waste session momentum.

**Why:** Surfaced 2026-06-23 during a template build. The library includes 334+ Diva presets and several Diva-focused sample packs (Datacode FOCUS, Melodic Techno Loops Vol.1-3, PML Production Pack Vol.2, EOS Deceiver Vol.6, Producer School Devotion) — but those are locked until the user buys a Diva license (~$179).

**How to apply:** For prog-house / melodic-techno work in Ableton, substitute Diva with:
- **Drift** (Live 12 stock) — closest analog character, best Diva swap for bass and lead roles
- **Analog** (Live 12 stock) — polyphonic pads
- **Wavetable** / **Meld** (Live 12 stock) — evolving textures
- **DUNE 3** / **Serum 2** (3rd-party, licensed) — wavetable/VA hybrid for modern prog leads

If the user mentions buying Diva, the [[project-music-library]] inventory of Diva presets becomes immediately useful again. Update SAMPLES.md and Ableton CLAUDE.md to remove the unlicensed warning at that point.
